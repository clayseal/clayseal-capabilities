# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people and assume they are being straight with you. Argue about
the work, not the person doing it. When you are wrong, say so and move on.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Specifics

Not acceptable: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, sexual
attention or imagery, publishing someone's private information, or sustained
disruption of a discussion.

Also not acceptable, and more common in a project like this one: dismissing a
report because of who filed it, restating a claim more loudly instead of
answering the objection, or treating a question about a number as an attack on
the person who produced it. This repository asks people to check its
measurements. That has to be safe to do.

## Reporting

Report to <peterberlizov@gmail.com>. Reports are read by the maintainers and not
shared further without your agreement, except where we are legally obliged.

If your report concerns a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled by someone
else.

We will acknowledge within three working days. We will tell you what we decided
and why. If we disagree with you, you will get a reason rather than silence.

## Consequences

In order, depending on severity and repetition: a private word, a public
correction, a temporary ban, a permanent ban. A first offence is not usually a
ban, and a pattern usually is.

## Security reports are different

A vulnerability report is not an issue. Do not open one publicly; see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
